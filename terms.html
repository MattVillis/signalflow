Your drawings

Your drawings are yours. SignalFlow runs in your browser and keeps your work there and in the files you save. We don't receive it, with these exceptions:

  • Live sessions: while you share a drawing in a live session, it's relayed and stored on SignalFlow's session server so everyone in the session sees the same thing. A session nobody has used for 30 days is deleted.
  • Anything you send us yourself, such as a symbol submission or a feedback message.

Auto-save keeps a copy in your browser to protect you against a closed tab. It isn't a backup: keep your own copies of your project files.

Fonts

You can set a drawing in a font of your choice, in two ways:

  • Free fonts are fetched by name from the Fontsource library through the jsDelivr network when a drawing uses them. They come from the Google Fonts catalogue and similar collections, and their authors publish them under open licences, mostly the SIL Open Font License. Each font's licence is on its own page; check it if you have particular needs.
  • Font files you load stay on your computer. SignalFlow doesn't upload them, doesn't store them anywhere else and doesn't put them in your project files. A project only names the font, so anyone else who opens it needs the font too, or sees the default font instead.

SignalFlow doesn't supply, sell or license fonts. Only load fonts you have the right to use. Font licences vary, and some limit how a font can be used, including whether it may be embedded in the PDFs you export or shared with other people. Making sure your use of a font you load follows its licence is your responsibility, not ours, and we accept no liability for it.

Symbols and suggestions you send in

Symbol submissions, symbol fixes and category suggestions are made as public issues on GitHub, under your GitHub account. Once the maintainer approves one, it's published in the shared symbol library and used by everyone who uses SignalFlow. Only send in symbols you made or are free to share; port lists taken from a manufacturer's published specifications are fine. Don't put anything private in them. The maintainer may edit, decline or later remove any submission.
