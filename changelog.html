2026-09-29

  • Ghosted kit is properly out of the way. Dragging a marquee, or Ctrl+A, no longer picks up equipment the ghost filter has faded. If you cannot see it you cannot select it, so it cannot be moved or deleted by accident either.
  • USB never blocks on direction. A USB socket is a socket: two ports that both read IN will still take a lead between them. The arrows stay on the drawing where they mean something — which way video or power flows on a USB-C port, or NovaStar cascade order — they just no longer stop you cabling. While checking that: NovaStar cascade USB IN is a Type-B socket and USB OUT is Type-A, per the VX1000 spec, so the VX1000, VX600 and MCTRL4K now say so.
  • USB cables just work. USB-A, USB-B and USB-C now connect to each other — an A-to-B or A-to-C lead is an ordinary cable, not a converter, and the app was wrongly asking for one. USB-B is a connector type in its own right now, so the ports that really are type B — the Taurus UCX host ports, the VINX service port — are drawn and listed as USB-B, and the cable list names both ends so the right lead gets ordered. USB-C into an HDMI or DisplayPort input is allowed too, flagged as needing that cable. Thanks to the beta user who reported it.

2026-09-28

  • What's new, in the app. This panel. The status bar carries a dot until the newest entry has been read, and the full history is a page on the site.
  • Ghost sheets. Alt+1 … Alt+6 — or Ctrl+Alt+V/F/N/A/C/P — fades everything that is not carrying that signal, so a busy sheet becomes just the network, or just the video. They stack, Esc clears, and alt-clicking a legend row does the same. A chip top-left names the filter.
  • It follows the signal through the kit. A fibre run is never a service of its own, so it carries whatever the box at its end terminates: focusing VIDEO keeps the fibre between a TX and an RX, focusing NETWORK keeps a fibre trunk carrying the LAN. An RJ45 is read off its port name — AV, TPS, TPX, SDVoE and NDI ports are an AV stream, DANTE is audio, plain LAN stays the network — so an AV-over-IP chain shows encoder, switch and decoder without the rest of the LAN lighting up. PoE counts as power.
  • Export and save by signal. The Export menu has a SIGNAL row: tick what you want and it drives the SVG, the Print/PDF and a new project copy holding only those cables and the equipment they touch. A normal Save now remembers the view you were in, without removing anything from the file.
  • Lightware, in full — 52 symbols. MX2 matrices including the audio and redundant frames and the DH DisplayPort hybrids, Taurus UCX across its generations, MMX multiport switchers, TPS / TPX / OPTX extenders, VINX, Gemini GVN and UBEX. Ports come from Lightware's own per-model port diagrams.
  • openGear frames. Ross OGX-FR and AJA OG-X-FR with an 18-card catalogue from AJA, Cobalt Digital and Ross. Cards are listed as card + rear-module pairings, because in openGear the rear module is what decides the connectors.
  • Power everywhere. Every symbol that takes mains now has an inlet, DC and 4-pin XLR are proper connector types, and the demo sheet ships with a 12-way distro cabled to everything. One fix worth naming: the disguise EX 3 was drawn with dual IEC — the whole VX/GX/EX range is a single powerCON TRUE1.

2026-09-25

  • VLAN subnets and automatic IPs. Give a VLAN a subnet and, with the setting on, anything you patch to it gets the next free address — shown under the symbol name, or under the port itself when a box has several LAN connections. Switches are skipped, and the VLAN is taken from either end of the cable.
  • Your VLAN scheme persists. "Save as defaults" keeps your numbering for every new project, and it travels inside the library file between machines.
  • Switch patch sheet. A third call list: every network port on every switch, its VLAN, what it connects to and the cable number. Spare ports are listed too, so it doubles as a free-capacity check.
  • KVM category with the ADDERLink XDIP and Matrox Avio 2, transmitter and receiver each.
  • Tidier library footer — the file actions became icons and the Repo button now carries its own sync status.

2026-09-24

  • Collapse a symbol to only the ports it is patched into — the [−] in the header, or C on a selection. Handy on a 48-port frame with six cables in it.
  • Slot presets for modular symbols: save "VX 4+ with HDMI VFCs", star it as the default for new placements, and it travels in the library file.
  • Client logo in the title block, with a small designer for its position, size and alignment. It reaches SVG and PDF as well as the screen.
  • Pixelhue and Analog Way packs, plus the Videohub 120x120 12G and the modular Universal Videohub 288.
  • Port limits lifted when building a symbol — a 288-port chassis is fine now.
  • Toolbar tidy; copy/paste/duplicate are keyboard only, and Ctrl+S saves.

2026-09-23

  • Feedback form in the status bar: bug, idea, missing symbol, with an optional diagnostics line. It never includes your drawing.

2026-09-22

  • LED processing: Brompton Tessera, Megapixel HELIOS and the NovaStar range, including the modular H-series.
  • PIXERA, LightAct and Blackmagic Design packs — cameras in their own category, the 2110 IP range and the rest of Videohub.
  • Auto-save to this browser, with a Restore card if the tab dies. Nothing leaves the machine.
  • Ctrl+F finds devices, ports and IPs across every sheet; page frames give a true A4/A3/A2 print and PDF, and the Export menu gathers page size, SVG and print in one place.
  • The library opens with categories folded, and two inputs or two outputs can no longer be cabled together.

2026-09-21

  • Editable cable styles: click a legend row to change a signal's colour, dash and name, and pick how crossings are drawn (none, arc or gap).
  • Link editing: double-click a cable to add a bend, drag either end dot onto a different port.
  • Port gaps for visual separation between port groups on a symbol.
  • Call lists: equipment and a numbered cable list, as CSV or a printable page.
  • Connector rules: two inputs cannot meet, DP↔HDMI is drawn as a gradient and flagged as needing a cable or dongle, anything else needs a converter. One cable per port, with a warning before you replace one.
  • Cross-sheet references: click a port, change sheet, click another — both sheets get a flag pointing at the other.
